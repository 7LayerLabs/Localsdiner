Locals Loyalty Rewards

Our way of saying thank you for being part of the family

Earn Rewards Every Visit

At Locals Diner, we believe loyalty should be rewarded. Our punch card program makes it easy to save while you enjoy the food you love.

Every time you dine with us and spend $10 or more, we'll punch your card. Collect 8 punches and you'll earn $10 off your next visit. It's that simple!

No apps to download. No accounts to create. Just great food and real rewards — the old-fashioned way.

How It Works

1

Get Your Card

Ask your server for a Locals Loyalty card on your next visit. It's free — no sign-up required!

2

Earn Punches

Every time you spend $10 or more, you'll earn a punch on your card. One punch per visit.

3

Get Rewarded

After 8 punches, you'll get $10 off your next meal! Then grab a new card and start again.

Ready to Start Earning?

Ask your server to sign you up on your next visit. No apps, no hassle — just real rewards for real locals.
